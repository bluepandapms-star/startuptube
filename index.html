About StartupTube

StartupTube is an open-source, community-driven library of the startup talks founders recommend most — on ideas, customers, sales, business psychology and fundraising. Every video is hand-picked, with a short note on why it's worth your time.

No account, no algorithm, no clickbait. Your likes, history and watch-later list stay in your own browser.

Every video plays through YouTube's official embedded player and follows YouTube's Terms of Service. StartupTube doesn't host, download or edit any video; views count toward the original creator's channel. It's an independent project, not affiliated with YouTube, Y Combinator, Stanford or any speaker.

The library brings together Y Combinator talks, Lenny's Podcast episodes, hand-picked classics, and the startup videos Hacker News commenters recommend again and again (from the open HackerTube dataset, hackertube.org/data, ODC-By 1.0). The code and the video list are open on GitHub. Know a talk every founder should watch? Suggest it there and the community can review it.
